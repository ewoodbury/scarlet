Scan grouped sums in place on a reused table

## Summary

Grouped partial sums scan each morsel as a `(start, length)` range of the input key and value columns, then compact into one `GroupTable` kept on the worker. After each partial, only the slots that morsel filled are cleared. `grow` rebuilds the slot list in first-seen order.

`ColumnPipeline.runSum` calls `GroupedSumKernel`. `PartialCombine` and `HashReduce.byKey` keep their current behaviour. The materialisation study measures the new partial entry point.

## Study

Harness: 1e7 rows, 1024 groups, batch size 8192, 1222 morsels. Checksum `49999995000000`.

| partial stage | before | after |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| p50, 1 thread | 52.75 ms | 36.70 ms |
| p50, 4 threads | 19.93 ms | 11.79 ms |
| allocation, 1 thread | 390 MB | 16.90 MB |

Element-path footprint stays 126 batches and 124 visits. The partial stage records 1222 batches and 1222 visits, one compacted partial and one scheduler task per morsel.
