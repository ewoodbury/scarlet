Time sum and groupedSum in DistCollectionStudy

Measurement only. `DistCollectionStudy` now times `sum` and `groupedSum` on primitive sources. The planner and the kernels are unchanged.

Same plans, columnar and row. Warmup 5, measure 11. p50 is sorted index 5 and p95 is sorted index 9. `alloc` is the mean `ThreadMXBean` bytes per query, sampled while the action runs. Machine: Apple M1 Pro, Homebrew OpenJDK 17.0.16, G1, in-process sbt, `-Xmx3g`. Full writeup: `notes/sparklet/kernel-benchmarks.md` (the log; the earlier sections are unchanged).

Narrow is `ofInts.filter(_ > 0).map(_ * 2).sum` at 1e6 rows, checksum `500354792612`. The chain is six `map(_ + 1)` then `sum` on that same array, checksum `238777139`. Grouped is `ofIntPairs.groupedSum` at 1e7 rows and 1,024 groups, checksum `49999995000000`.

Six maps are past the four-step fusion window: a four-add column is written, and the last two adds fold into the sum. At one thread that is 11.2 ms and 20.8 MB, about 2.5× the fused narrow sum.

The row `groupedSum` of a bare `ofIntPairs` source adds values on the calling thread and boxes each pair. One thread and four threads match, at 85.5 ms and 940 MB. The totals matched the checksum. Left as-is.

Every sample matched its checksum. No engine bug turned up.
