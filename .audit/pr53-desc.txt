Add a materialisation study for the columnar operator boundary

`MaterialisationStudy` measures the columnar materialisation boundary. It is a `main`. `make test` does not run it. No production path changes.

F4 is four `map(_ + 1)` calls and `sum` (`Pipeline4(Scan)`): one encode, one slice, no output column. P5 is five maps and `sum` (`Project(Pipeline4(Scan))`): the pipeline writes a column, the morsels are concatenated, and the sum slices that column again. RAW is four dependent adds over the generator array, with no planner. GROUPED is `groupedSum` at 1e7 rows and 1024 groups. Its stage rows replay the public calls separately.

1e6 ints, 2 source partitions, batch 8192 (124 morsels). GROUPED uses 1,222 morsels and shuffle width 4. Warmup 5, measure 11. The oracle is checked outside the timer. `alloc` on query and stage rows is a sampled `ThreadMXBean` lower bound. `batches` is `ColumnMetrics` during the timed sample. One sitting on an Apple M1 Pro, OpenJDK 17.0.16, G1, `-Xmx3g`.

Checksums: F4 and RAW `236777139`, P5 `237777139`, GROUPED `49999995000000`.

Caller probes, one thread, not a split of a query total:

| probe | p50 | alloc | batches |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| encode | 0.143 ms | 4.13 MB | 2 |
| slice | 0.209 ms | 4.17 MB | 124 |
| output | 2.731 ms | 4.05 MB | 124 |
| concat | 0.225 ms | 4.16 MB | 2 |
| reslice | 0.215 ms | 4.17 MB | 124 |

F4 batches (126) match encode plus slice. P5 batches (376) match those plus output, concat, and reslice. The three extra probes sum to 12.37 MB. The sampled P5−F4 allocation delta is 12.41 MB at one thread and 9.46 MB at four.

Against RAW:

- F4 has no second materialisation pass. It is 3.79× RAW at one thread and 4.38× RAW at four.
- P5's extra pass is visible: +250 batches and +124 visits. P5−F4 is 0.821 ms at one thread (1.24× RAW, 24.6% of P5) and 1.157 ms at four threads (3.73× RAW, 46.0% of P5).
- GROUPED is 7.32 ns/row at one thread and 3.55 ns/row at four. RAW is 0.66 ns/row and 0.31 ns/row on 1e6 adds. That pair is not a boundary ratio.

Threshold comparisons, p50, no redesign chosen:

- One third of P5 is 1.112 ms at one thread and 0.837 ms at four. P5−F4 is 0.74× that line at one thread and 1.38× it at four. The extra batches are the written column, the concat, and the second slice.
- Twice RAW is 1.329 ms at one thread and 0.619 ms at four. F4 is above that line at both widths, so the F4-to-RAW gap is in the fused path.
- 15% of P5 is 0.500 ms at one thread and 0.377 ms at four. P5−F4 is above that line at both widths.
